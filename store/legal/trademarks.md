# Trademark and naming guidance

- Say **"works with AutoCAD"**, never "supported by Autodesk". Autodesk does not certify virtual machines or Windows on ARM; state that plainly wherever AutoCAD is mentioned in store listings and docs.
- Use competitor names only in factual comparisons with sources, never in app names, icons, keywords fields that imply affiliation, or ad headlines (App Store keyword policy and Google Play impersonation policy).
- "Windows" is Microsoft's trademark: "Run Windows software" is fine; "Windows for Mac" as a product name is not. Never imply Windows is included.
- Do not use Apple's product images or the App Store badge outside Apple's marketing guidelines; use the official badges from Apple and Google unchanged.
- MIRRORZ wordmark: all caps in marketing, gradient mark (cyan to violet). File a trademark search before launch in the US (USPTO class 9 and 42) and the EU; "Mirrorz"-like marks in software should be cleared by counsel.
